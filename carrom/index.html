Carrom

How to play
  • Slide the striker along your baseline, then drag back anywhere on the board and release to flick it. A longer drag is a harder strike.
  • White plays from the bottom, black from the top. Pocket one of your own coins and you strike again.
  • The red queen must be covered: pocket one of your coins on the same strike or the next one, or the queen goes back to the centre.
  • Pocketing the striker is a foul. Coins you pocketed on that strike come back, plus one more as a penalty.
  • You can't pocket your last coin until the queen has been covered.
  • Clear all your coins to win. You score one point per coin your opponent has left, plus 3 if you covered the queen.
